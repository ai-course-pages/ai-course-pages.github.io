What AI means in this course

This page fixes the definition and the words around it. Later items go into data, uses, and methods. You can open those items before this one feels finished.

Artificial intelligence (AI) is the ability of computer programs and robots to mimic human intelligence.

People meet that ability in work, in education, and in everyday life. This course looks at it mainly as something in society: what it does in daily life, and what follows from using it.

Words this course keeps

  • Human intelligence is the set of human abilities named in the definition, such as recognizing something, using language, or solving a problem.
  • Data is the information an AI system is given or produces. A later item covers why quality and selection matter.
  • Application is a place AI is used. The first overview in this course names prediction, robotics, computer vision, and generative AI.
  • Technique is a kind of method. The first overview names search, classification, and object recognition.
  • Algorithm is a specific procedure. The first overview names decision trees, regression, and supervised and unsupervised learning.
  • Machine learning is one family of methods for training a system from data. In this course it sits inside AI, as one family among others.
