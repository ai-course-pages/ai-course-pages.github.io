Compress or compact a thread you are staying in

A long thread resends its earlier turns. Most products shorten that thread on their own as the context window fills toward its limit. The shortening is a compression or a compaction (other designations could possibly occasionally be used to describe the concept too). What remains is a shorter text that keeps the decisions and the open task, so later turns resend that text instead of the trail that produced it.

The page before this one, U1.S.2, is for opening a new thread. This page is for a thread you are still using. You watch the product do the pass. You do not have to request it.

If these words are new

A learner with little background would want U1.B.1 first. A follow-up resends the turns the product still keeps. A compression or compaction is how a long thread gets cheaper while remaining the same chat.

When the product does it

Some products wait until the window is nearly full. Others start earlier, once a threshold is crossed. Among the ones that start earlier, a few begin at roughly four fifths of the window, or a comparable share. You may see that share on some products. The product chooses the moment.

How long the thread is, and how much material it holds, can move the moment. The amount that matters follows how the text is tokenized. The size of an attached file, in bytes, is a different measure.

How you see it

Many products show a line in the interface while the pass runs. The line often says Compressing or Compacting. It often includes a percent for how far the pass is estimated to have gotten, and sometimes a line that asks you to wait. The pass usually takes a noticeable amount of time. Leave the thread open until that line finishes.

What you hope it kept

  • Keep decisions that still bind, names, paths, and the open task.
  • Drop dead ends, repeated attempts, and steps already saved in a file.

After the line finishes, ask what context is still in the thread. A compression or compaction you can read can later become the seed you paste into a new thread. Until then it is the standing text of the chat you are in. The model stays the same.

Suggested exercise

The next time you have a long thread you are willing to watch, stay in it and keep working in the ordinary way until the product compresses or compacts on its own. If you continue mainly to see this happen, use a thread you are willing to experiment with.

When the interface has shown Compressing or Compacting, and the pass has finished, one of these is a fit for the next message.

I noticed you just compressed or compacted this thread. What context do you still have?
Which decisions are you still treating as binding, and what is the open task?
What did the compression or compaction leave out?
