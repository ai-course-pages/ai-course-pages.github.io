Welcome to this section

This part of the course is about spending a model's time on purpose. You will choose how hard it thinks, notice when a thread gets expensive, read a usage cap, shorten a thread you are staying in, and leave a chat with a short seed or a reusable skill instead of the whole transcript.

The greeting at the start of the whole course is a longer one. It is for someone meeting a model for the first time. This welcome is only for this section. You can take the section in order, or come to it on its own.

If you have been moving through the course

The opening that sits before this section, in the plan, is where a newcomer meets three ideas. A model reads a prompt and writes a reply. The text you send, the hidden work, and the reply are counted separately. Changing a setting does not swap in a different model. Those pages are not all written yet. The ideas are the ground this section uses.

Another part of the course is the subject itself: what artificial intelligence refers to, in ordinary work and daily life. That subject does not have to come first. Nothing here depends on it.

If you are taking this section on its own

If you have not completed the previous parts, or you are doing this section standalone, this section assumes that you can at least send a prompt and read a reply, and that you know the text you send, the hidden work, and the reply are counted separately. It also assumes you know that a setting is not a different model.
