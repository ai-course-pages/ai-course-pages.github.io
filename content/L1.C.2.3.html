Which part is used

Quality, whether the data fits the use, is on L1.C.2.2.

Data selection is the choice of which available data the system will use. The rest stays out. The result follows the part that was used, which is why the choice matters as much as the size of the pile.

The park app may have a year of entrance counts, photos from three gates, and posts. Someone, or some step in the system, then keeps only the morning counts from the main gate. The question was about the afternoon, and about the whole park. The answer can be careful and still describe a morning at one gate.

A pool, and a subset

Try it on six notes

The question is whether the park feels safe at night. These six notes are the pool. Choose a subset you would actually give the system, and name one thing your subset leaves out.

  1. A noon photo of the playground, full of families.
  2. A night photo of the main path, with the lamps on and two people walking.
  3. A night photo of the same path, lamps off, no people visible.
  4. A post about a lost dog, sent at noon.
  5. A post from a neighbor: "I cut across at 11pm and the path was empty."
  6. Last July's crowd count for the afternoon concert.

There is more than one defensible subset. The check is whether you can say what the subset is, and what it cannot speak for. A subset of noon photos can support a sunny-day description. It cannot support a night answer.
