Data, quality, and selection, together

This page adds no new idea. It puts the three pages in one place so you can see the order.

  1. Data is the information an AI system is given or produces. Counts, photos, posts, and the labels the system writes are data. The wish for a good answer is not.
  2. Quality asks whether that data fits the stated use. Missing weekends, winter photos for a summer question, jokes in place of reports, and a pile too thin to show the afternoon are failures of fit. A real file can still be unfit.
  3. Selection is which part of the available data is used. The result follows that subset. Morning counts from one gate answer a different question than the whole park in the afternoon.

The park question ties them. Name the information. Ask whether it fits "this afternoon." Then say which part was kept. The answer the app gives is about that kept part.

Choosing what an assistant reads, while a course page is written, is the same kind of selection. The worked example is on Methods and algorithms, in a new tab.
