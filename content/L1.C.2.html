Data, quality, and selection

You can read L1.C.1 first. That page names data as the information an AI system is given or produces. This group stays with that word.

An AI system works from information. The result follows the information it is given, and the part of that information that is actually used. Three questions keep that straight.

  1. L1.C.2.1 What counts as data.
  2. L1.C.2.2 Quality: whether that data fits the use.
  3. L1.C.2.3 Selection: which part is used, and what that leaves out.

L1.C.2.9 is the summary. It adds no new idea.

One case runs through the three pages. A neighborhood app answers "is the park busy this afternoon?" The pages look at the information that question depends on.
