# Aims

id	text
S1	Artificial intelligence (AI) is the ability of computer programs and robots to mimic human intelligence. People use it in work, education, and everyday life.
S2	The course treats AI development mainly from a societal perspective and looks at implications for daily life.
S3	Students learn key concepts and techniques and get an introduction and overview of AI and its applications.
S4	Students see how AI affects daily life and how people perceive the world around them: underlying mechanisms, primary components, and structure.
S5	Students learn the laws and other regulations that matter for AI, and practice legal, ethical, economic, and security considerations.
S6	Students examine opportunities and consequences of the technology, including how it can contribute to sustainable development.
S7	Students compare similarities and differences between human and artificial intelligence.
S8	Each level alternates theory and practice. Students use digital tools, software, and programming, and turn that knowledge into action on a problem.
S9	Students discuss ethical issues and alternative courses of action.
