## How a page is shaped

- Prose, or a video later, carries the idea. One page, a few questions at most inside the prose.
- The bottom is a quiz of three or four choices. It is a first-reading check. It can be clicked through without learning, so an iteration does not wait on it.
- Suggested exercises are the part to try in the working chat. They are how the next iteration is chosen.
- If the reader already knows the page, they say so and name what to learn or revisit. That may be a hole in the table above, a later idea, or a rewrite of this page.
